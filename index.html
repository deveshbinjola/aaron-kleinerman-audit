We don't just create content. We build the machine that creates it, the workflows that run it, and the SOPs that mean one person (or one AI) can keep it going after we leave.

📅 Content Calendar SOP

  • Weekly posting rhythm tied to retreat schedule
  • Monday: teaching carousel (Masculine Awakening)
  • Tuesday: embodiment reel (breathwork, practice)
  • Thursday: relationship / love carousel
  • Friday: shadow / truth-bomb reel
  • Sunday: podcast clip repurpose
  • Each piece traced to a content pillar + revenue goal

🎧 Podcast-to-Content Pipeline

  • Episode drops → auto-transcribed (free)
  • AI extracts 3-5 quotable moments per episode
  • Quotes routed to carousel templates
  • Key clips cut into 30-60s reels
  • Blog post drafted from transcript themes
  • One episode = 8-10 content pieces

✈️ Retreat Launch Playbook

  • 12-week launch sequence per retreat
  • Email drip: 8 emails from announcement to close
  • Social campaign: countdown carousels + reels
  • Testimonial assets from past retreaters
  • Urgency triggers: early-bird, limited spots, alumni pricing
  • Reusable for every retreat, every location

📈 Lead Magnet Funnel

  • Landing page on aaronkleinerman.com
  • Downloadable guide (from your content pillars)
  • Ontraport email capture + tag
  • 5-email welcome sequence in your voice
  • Soft CTA to retreat or free masterclass
  • Automated, runs 24/7, fills your list while you sleep

🕵️ Brand Consolidation

  • Audit all 5+ domains for redundancy
  • Redirect strategy: funnel all traffic to one hub
  • Cross-linking architecture for SEO juice
  • Unified visual identity across platforms
  • One "brand bible" your team can follow
  • One domain. One authority. One searchable brand.

🤖 AI-Assisted (Human-Led)

  • AI drafts in your voice (from voice library)
  • You or your person reviews & approves
  • Nothing posts without human eyes
